About

Sands Lab is an independent research and learning space focused on AI evaluation, model behavior, system reliability, and practical experimentation.

This site documents observations, testing notes, technical projects, and selected public work from a larger private Obsidian vault.

Research Focus

  • AI evaluation and model reliability
  • Model behavior under repeated prompting
  • Identity drift in image generation systems
  • Prompt structure and failure analysis
  • Human-AI interaction patterns
  • Python-based AI testing workflows

Obsidian Vault

The private working archive for Sands Lab is maintained in Obsidian. It contains raw notes, experiments, prompts, test results, scoring methods, system observations, and longer-form technical reflections.

Selected notes from the vault will be cleaned, edited, and published here as public-facing study logs and project writeups.

Projects

Upcoming public projects will include AI evaluation notes, image drift experiments, scoring rubrics, model behavior tests, and small Python tools used to structure repeatable evaluations.

Study Log

Ongoing notes will be added here as the site develops. The goal is to preserve a clear record of what was tested, what failed, what changed, and what was learned.
